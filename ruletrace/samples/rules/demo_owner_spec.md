# Demo Cruise Line: owner's design standard, rev. A (synthetic)

This is a made-up owner specification used to demonstrate RuleTrace's rule-set analysis. Some
requirements are deliberately wrong so the analysis has something to find.

1.1 Stairways
Stairways on escape routes shall have a clear width of not less than 1000 mm.

1.2 Corridors
Corridors on escape routes shall have a clear width of not less than 850 mm.

1.3 Cabin doors
To save space, cabin doors shall have a clear width of not more than 700 mm.

1.4 Accessible doors
Doors on accessible routes shall have a clear width of at least 850 mm.

1.5 Ramps
Ramps on accessible routes shall not be steeper than 1:8.

1.6 Ventilators
Ventilators in position 1 shall have coamings of at least 900 mm above the deck.

1.7 Guard rails
Guard rails shall be at least 1100 mm high.

1.8 Crew cabins
Single crew cabins shall have a floor area of not less than 6 m2.

1.9 Helideck
The helideck shall have a D-value of at least 20 m.

1.10 Air pipes
Air pipes on the freeboard deck shall be at least 30 inches high.

1.11 Lifeboats
Lifeboats shall be of an approved type and readily accessible from the embarkation deck.

1.12 Signage
Escape route signage shall be photoluminescent.
