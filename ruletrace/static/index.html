How RuleTrace works
  1. Load a ship design: a GA drawing (PDF), an IFC 3D model, or one of our samples.
  2. See the problems on the model: parts that break a rule turn red.
  3. Click a problem to see the rule, what was required, what the design has, and how to fix it.
